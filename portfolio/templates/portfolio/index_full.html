Case study 03

Location analytics & gravity models

Gravity (Huff) models that forecast customer traffic for a new retail location against its competitors. The grid is built on residential data, so the result is the number of households in the catchment, weighted by their probability of visiting. For a new 50,000 m² shopping centre in Kaunas, the model estimated about 103,000 regular visitors, around 29,000 a day.
